Frequently asked questions

How does Littlebird understand my work?

Littlebird learns by seeing what you see. It pays attention to the active window on your screen, like the document you're writing or the website you're browsing. During meetings, it listens along to take notes for you. It can also connect to the tools you already use, like your email and calendar, to pull in context and take actions on your behalf.

Do I need to connect all my apps for Littlebird to work?

No, and that's what makes Littlebird so powerful. It works automatically by seeing what's on your screen, giving you a unified memory across all your apps without any setup. We also offer hundreds of integrations with your favorite apps so you can give Littlebird deeper access to the tools you use most. For example, if you want Littlebird to help schedule meetings, you can integrate with Google Calendar.

What integrations does Littlebird support?

Littlebird supports hundreds of integrations across a wide range of categories, including:

Project management: Notion, Linear, ClickUp, monday.com, Atlassian Rovo (Jira and Confluence)

Design and collaboration: Canva, Miro, Lucid

Developer tools: Axiom, Cloudflare Developer Platform, PlanetScale, Honeycomb

CRM and sales: Intercom, Outreach, Close, Clarify, Day AI

Finance: Mercury, Ramp, PayPal, Stripe

Meetings: Calendly, Fireflies, Granola, Krisp, Circleback

Marketing: Klaviyo, MailerLite, Bitly, Ahrefs, AirOps

Analytics: Mixpanel, Omni Analytics, PostHog

Knowledge and docs: Guru, Mem, Craft, Egnyte, Google Drive

Email and calendar: Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar, Apple Reminders

Integrations are optional. Littlebird works with every app automatically by observing your screen. But connecting your tools gives Littlebird even deeper access. You can browse the full list in Settings > Integrations.

Why should I use Littlebird instead of a general AI assistant or chatbot?

General AI assistants have no context about your work. You have to catch them up by copying and pasting information, which is slow and raises privacy concerns. Littlebird already understands the work behind your requests—your documents, conversations, and projects—so it gives you more relevant answers and stronger starting points. This makes Littlebird faster, more personal, and far more useful for day-to-day work.

Is my data secure with Littlebird?

Security is foundational to how Littlebird works. Everything you see is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-leading practices. You decide what Littlebird can access, what it keeps, and what it forgets. You can review and delete your data or your account at any time.

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored securely in the cloud on encrypted infrastructure. Access is limited to what's needed to operate the service, and you remain in control of what Littlebird can see and remember.

Do you use my data to train AI models or sell it to third parties?

No. We never sell your data, and we don't use it to train our models. Your information is yours — Littlebird simply helps you use it better.

What platforms or devices is Littlebird available on?

Littlebird is available for Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android. Download the desktop app for the full experience, and take Littlebird with you everywhere with the mobile apps.

Do you offer a student discount?

Yes! We offer a discount for students. Reach out to our support team with proof of enrollment and we'll get you set up.
