Frequently asked questions

Is this included in my plan?

Gmail and Google Calendar are included free on the Basic plan. Everything else — Notion, Asana, GitHub, Linear, ClickUp, Todoist, TickTick, and the rest — comes with Littlebird Plus. See pricing for details.

+
Is my data secure?

Yes. We treat your data with the utmost care and protect it using best-in-class industry security practices. Your information is always encrypted when stored on our servers, and any connection between Littlebird and third-party services you authorize is also encrypted in transit. We're SOC 2 certified — our security practices are regularly audited by an independent third party. We're also GDPR and CCPA compliant. You can delete your data at any time — all of it, or just recent data like the last hour or day.

+
I use a tool that isn't listed here. Do you support it?

Probably — the grid above shows our most-used integrations, not the full list. Littlebird connects to many more tools, and new integrations ship every week. See all integrations for the current list.
