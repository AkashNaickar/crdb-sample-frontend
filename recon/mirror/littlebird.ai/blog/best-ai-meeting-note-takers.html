The best AI meeting note takers in 2026

Carson Eisner
Growth Engineer

Littlebird is the best AI meeting note taker for people whose meetings are part of a larger workday, because it transcribes calls with no bot and connects the notes to a running memory of everything else you see and do. Granola is the best pick if you want a focused, template-driven meeting notepad and nothing more.

Who is ranking whom: this site is published by Littlebird, and Littlebird appears in this ranking. We put it first where we believe the fit is real, we explain why in plain terms, and every entry ends with an honest reason to choose it instead. Competitor descriptions are verified against public materials and dated in the text.

How we ranked these five tools

We ranked on three things you can verify in a single trial call: whether a bot joins it, what happens to the notes after it ends, and whether the tool helps with the work around the meeting or only the meeting itself. Price tiers change often, so check each vendor's site for current numbers.

1. Littlebird: notes that become part of a bigger memory

Littlebird is an AI assistant that already knows your work. During meetings it listens along to transcribe and summarize, and no bot joins the call. It also works for any audio or video playing on your computer, not only live calls, so recorded webinars and screen shares get captured too.

The difference shows up after the call. Littlebird pays attention to the active window you are working in, so the call notes land next to the email thread, the doc, and the plan they belong to. Ask Chat what you promised a client last month and the answer comes from your own history, not from a search box.

Be aware of the tradeoff: the app runs on your computer, and the memory it builds is encrypted and stored in the AWS cloud. If you need a local-only tool, Littlebird's individual plans are not that today.

Choose Granola or a dedicated meeting tool instead if the notes are all you want and you would rather not have an assistant that pays attention to the rest of your screen.

2. Granola: the focused, bot-free notepad

Granola is a focused, bot-free meeting notetaker with customizable templates, and its notes are shaped by what you jot during the call (verified June 2026). It raised a $125M Series C at a $1.5B valuation in March 2026, so it is a well-resourced, serious product.

Choose Granola when you want a dedicated meeting tool that stays out of the way, and you like guiding the summary with your own quick notes.

3. Fathom: fast summaries with CRM sync

Fathom is a meeting tool with a generous free plan and CRM sync, and it uses a meeting bot by default (verified June 2026). For sales teams that live in a CRM, that sync is the point.

Choose Fathom when you want fast summaries pushed into your CRM, or team features like shared call libraries and coaching scorecards.

4. Otter: transcription with team features

Otter is a focused meeting and transcription tool that joins your call as a visible participant (verified June 2026). It is one of the longest-running products in the category and is built around team collaboration on transcripts.

Choose Otter when you want a team workspace for meeting transcripts and you do not mind attendees seeing a bot in the participant list.

5. Cluely: live help during the call

Cluely is a real-time, in-meeting assistant built for live performance on calls, especially sales calls (verified June 2026). It is a different bet than a note taker: help in the moment rather than a record afterward.

Choose Cluely when you want live coaching during sales calls rather than notes and memory afterward.

The tools that skip the meeting bot

Littlebird and Granola take notes with no bot in the call, while Otter joins as a visible participant and Fathom uses a bot by default (all verified June 2026). If the bot question decides it for you, we wrote a separate guide to bot-free note takers.

  • Littlebird. Bot in the call? No. Scope: Meetings plus your whole workday. Best for: Notes connected to a work memory.
  • Granola. Bot in the call? No. Scope: Calls only. Best for: Template-driven notes you shape as you jot.
  • Fathom. Bot in the call? Yes, by default. Scope: Calls only. Best for: Sales teams that need CRM sync.
  • Otter. Bot in the call? Yes, visible participant. Scope: Calls only. Best for: Team collaboration on transcripts.
  • Cluely. Bot in the call? In-call assistant. Scope: During the call. Best for: Live coaching on sales calls.

Common questions

Why not just use ChatGPT for meeting notes?

General assistants have no context. You copy and paste to catch them up, which is slow and raises privacy concerns. Littlebird already understands the work behind your request, and it captured the meeting automatically.

Does Littlebird's transcription put a bot in my meeting?

No. It listens along on your computer to transcribe and summarize, and no bot joins the call. It also works for any audio or video playing on your computer.

Is my data secure with Littlebird?

Yes. Encrypted at rest and in transit, SOC 2 certified, GDPR-aligned and CCPA-aligned data handling, delete anytime.

What platforms does Littlebird run on?

Mac and Windows, plus iOS and Android companion apps. The core product is desktop.
